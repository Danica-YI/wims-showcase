WIMS: Warehouse Inventory Management System

A role-based web app for running a small warehouse: track stock, record goods in and out, catch low stock before it runs out, and take purchase orders from draft to delivered.

Everything it does

Sign-in and roles Admin + Staff

  • Secure login with JSON Web Tokens; passwords are hashed with bcrypt.
  • Admins and staff get different dashboards and menus.
  • Admin-only actions are enforced by the API, not just hidden in the UI.

Stock management

  • Add, edit and delete products with SKU, category, unit, price and a low-stock threshold.
  • Search the catalogue as you type.
  • Quantities update automatically from sales, deliveries and approved adjustments.

Goods in and out Staff

  • Record sales and supplier returns by SKU; the product name fills in automatically.
  • An outbound is blocked if there isn't enough stock.
  • Inbound lists shipped orders waiting to be received.

Low-stock alerts Admin acts

  • Raised automatically when a product falls to its threshold.
  • One open alert per product, so they never pile up.
  • Admins reorder or ignore, with a note; every outcome is kept.

Purchase orders Admin only

  • Draft → Pending → Approved → Shipped → Received, with a progress bar.
  • A carrier and tracking number are required before shipping.
  • Receiving an order adds its items to stock.

Suppliers Admin only

  • Contact details and product categories for every supplier.
  • Search, add, edit and delete suppliers.

Stock adjustments Staff request · Admin approves

User management Admin only

  • See every account with its role and status.
  • Promote staff to admin, or back, from a dropdown.

Global search

  • Search products, orders and suppliers at once from the dashboard.
  • Each result jumps straight to the matching record.
